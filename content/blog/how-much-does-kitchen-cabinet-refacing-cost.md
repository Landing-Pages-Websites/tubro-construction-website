<h2><span>What is Cabinet Refacing and How Does it Work?</span></h2>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Cabinet refacing updates your kitchen by replacing the visible parts of your cabinets. </span></p><p><br/></p><p><span>Instead of removing the entire structure, the cabinet frames remain in place, while doors, drawer fronts, and hardware are replaced. The exposed surfaces of the frames are covered with a matching finish for a cohesive look.</span></p><p><br/></p><p><span>Here’s how the process works, with cost estimates for each step:</span></p><p><br/></p> <ol><li><strong>Remove Doors and Drawer Fronts</strong><span> <span>($200–$500 for labor): This step involves carefully removing the old doors, drawer fronts, and hardware. The cost depends on the size of your kitchen and the contractor’s labor rates.<br/><br/></span></span></li><li><strong>Prepare the Cabinet Frames</strong><span> <span>($500–$1,000 for materials and labor): The cabinet frames are cleaned, repaired, and sanded to ensure the new finish adheres properly. Minor repairs, such as filling in cracks or smoothing rough areas, are also done during this step.<br/><br/></span></span></li><li><strong>Apply New Finish</strong><span> <span>($1,500–$3,000 for materials and labor): A new veneer or laminate is applied to the cabinet frames. The cost varies based on the type of finish you choose, such as wood veneer, laminate, or thermofoil.<br/><br/></span></span></li><li><strong>Install New Doors and Hardware</strong><span> <span>($2,000–$5,000 for materials and labor): New doors and drawer fronts are installed to match the updated frames. The cost includes the price of the doors, hardware like handles and knobs, and the labor to install them.</span></span></li></ol>
<p><br/></p><p><span>These costs are averages and may vary depending on the size of your kitchen, the materials you choose, and local labor rates. </span></p><p><br/></p><p><span>Refacing is still significantly more affordable than replacing cabinets entirely, which can cost $12,000 or more.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p>
