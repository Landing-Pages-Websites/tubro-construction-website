<p><span>Replacing kitchen cabinets can completely change the look of your kitchen, but it’s not cheap. Knowing what affects the cost can help you plan your budget.</span></p><p><strong><br/>On average, replacing kitchen cabinets costs between $3,000 and $12,000</strong><span>, depending on your kitchen’s size, materials, and labor. High-end custom cabinets can cost $25,000 or more.</span></p><p><span><br/>This guide will explain what impacts cabinet replacement costs, the types of cabinets available, and tips to save money. Let’s help you make the best choice for your home.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p>
<h2><span>What Factors Affect the Cost of Replacing Kitchen Cabinets?</span></h2>
<h3><span><br/></span></h3>
<h3><span>Kitchen Size</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>The size of your kitchen plays a big role in the cost of replacing cabinets. Larger kitchens need more cabinets, which increases both material and labor costs. Here's a breakdown:</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><strong>Small Kitchens (10x10)</strong><span><span>: Replacing cabinets in a small kitchen typically costs</span>
</span><strong>$3,000 to $6,000</strong><span>.</span></li><li><strong>Medium Kitchens (12x12)</strong><span><span>: For medium-sized kitchens, expect to pay</span>
</span><strong>$6,000 to $12,000</strong><span>.</span></li><li><strong>Large Kitchens (200+ sq. ft.)</strong><span><span>: Costs can go up to</span>
</span><strong>$15,000 or more</strong><span>, especially with high-end materials or custom designs.</span></li></ul>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>The more cabinets you need, the higher the total cost. </span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <h3><span>How Do Materials Influence the Cost to Replace?</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>The material you choose for your cabinets has a significant impact on the total cost. Here's what you can expect:</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><strong>Laminate Cabinets</strong><span>:<br/><br/></span></li><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $50–$200 per linear foot</span></li><li><strong>Overview</strong><span>: A budget-friendly option that is durable and easy to clean. However, laminate may not last as long as other materials.<br/><br/></span></li><li><strong>Medium-Density Fiberboard (MDF)</strong><span>:<br/><br/></span></li><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $100–$300 per linear foot</span></li><li><strong>Overview</strong><span>: Offers a smooth surface for painted finishes at a mid-range price. It’s less durable than solid wood but resistant to cracking.<br/><br/></span></li><li><strong>Solid Wood Cabinets</strong><span>:<br/><br/></span></li><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $200–$500 per linear foot</span></li><li><strong>Overview</strong><span>: A classic choice for its durability and natural look. Prices vary based on wood type, with options like oak, maple, and cherry.<br/><br/></span></li><li><strong>Thermofoil Cabinets</strong><span>:<br/><br/></span></li><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $150–$400 per linear foot</span></li><li><strong>Overview</strong><span>: Features a plastic coating over MDF for a sleek, uniform finish. It’s easy to maintain but can warp under high heat.<br/><br/></span></li><li><strong>Stainless Steel Cabinets</strong><span>:<br/><br/></span></li><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $400–$1,200 per linear foot</span></li><li><strong>Overview</strong><span>: Known for a modern, professional kitchen look, stainless steel is durable but comes with a higher price tag.</span></li></ul>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Choosing the right material depends on your budget and the style you want for your kitchen. Solid wood is great for durability, while laminate is ideal for saving money.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <h3><span>What Role Do Labor Costs Play in the Total Cost?</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span><span>Labor costs are a significant part of replacing kitchen cabinets, often making up</span>
</span><strong>40% to 60%</strong><span> <span>of the total expense. These costs cover tasks like removing old cabinets, preparing the space, and installing the new ones. Here’s a breakdown of what to expect:</span></span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><strong>Cabinet Removal</strong><span>:</span></li><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $300–$1,000</span></li><li><strong>Details</strong><span>: This includes removing and disposing of old cabinets. Costs may increase if the cabinets are glued or nailed in place.</span></li><li><strong>Installation</strong><span>:</span></li><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $50–$250 per cabinet or $2,000–$6,000 for the entire kitchen.</span></li><li><strong>Details</strong><span>: Labor-intensive installations, such as custom or inset cabinets, are priced higher due to the precise work required.</span></li><li><strong>Additional Tasks</strong><span>:</span></li><li><span><span>If your project requires electrical or plumbing adjustments, expect to pay</span>
</span><strong>$150–$500</strong><span> <span>for each service.</span></span></li></ul>
<p><span><br/></span></p>
<h2><span>How Much Does It Cost to Install New Kitchen Cabinets?</span></h2>
<h4><span><br/></span></h4>
<h3><span>What Is the Average Cost Per Cabinet?</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span><span>The cost to install kitchen cabinets is typically calculated on a per-cabinet basis. On average, the installation cost ranges from</span>
</span><strong>$50 to $250 per cabinet</strong><span>, depending on the type and complexity of the installation. Here's what you can expect:</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><strong>Stock Cabinets</strong><span><span>: Installation costs are lower, around</span>
</span><strong>$50 to $100 per cabinet</strong><span>, as they are pre-assembled and easy to install.</span></li><li><strong>Semi-Custom Cabinets</strong><span><span>: These require more adjustments, with installation costs ranging from</span>
</span><strong>$100 to $200 per cabinet</strong><span>.</span></li><li><strong>Custom Cabinets</strong><span><span>: Due to their bespoke nature, installation costs range from</span>
</span><strong>$200 to $250 or more per cabinet</strong><span>.</span></li></ul>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span><span>The total installation cost for an average kitchen usually falls between</span>
</span><strong>$2,000 and $6,000</strong><span>, depending on the number of cabinets and the complexity of the project.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <h3><span>What Is the Average Cost Per Cabinet?</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span><span>The cost to install kitchen cabinets is typically calculated on a per-cabinet basis. On average, the installation cost ranges from</span>
</span><strong>$50 to $250 per cabinet</strong><span>, depending on the type and complexity of the installation. Here's what you can expect:</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><strong>Stock Cabinets</strong><span><span>: Installation costs are lower, around</span>
</span><strong>$50 to $100 per cabinet</strong><span>, as they are pre-assembled and easy to install.</span></li><li><strong>Semi-Custom Cabinets</strong><span><span>: These require more adjustments, with installation costs ranging from</span>
</span><strong>$100 to $200 per cabinet</strong><span>.</span></li><li><strong>Custom Cabinets</strong><span><span>: Due to their bespoke nature, installation costs range from</span>

<h2><span>What Are the Cost Differences Between Cabinet Types?</span></h2>
<h4><span><br/></span></h4>
<h3><span>How Much Do Stock Cabinets Cost?</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Stock cabinets are pre-manufactured and ready to install, making them the most affordable option. These cabinets are available in standard sizes and limited designs.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $100–$300 per linear foot</span></li><li><strong>Details</strong><span>: Stock cabinets are mass-produced, which keeps costs low. They’re ideal for quick installations and budget-friendly kitchen updates.</span></li><li><strong>Best for</strong><span>: Homeowners on a tight budget or those remodeling rental properties.</span></li></ul>
<p><span><br/></span></p> <h3><span>What Are the Costs for Semi-Custom Cabinets?</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Semi-custom cabinets provide more design flexibility than stock cabinets, allowing for some adjustments in size, finish, and features.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $150–$650 per linear foot</span></li><li><strong>Details</strong><span>: Semi-custom cabinets are partially pre-made but offer customization options like additional shelving or unique finishes.</span></li><li><strong>Best for</strong><span>: Homeowners who want a balance between affordability and personalization.</span></li></ul>
<p><span><br/></span></p> <h3><span>Why Are Custom Cabinets More Expensive?</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Custom cabinets are built to your exact specifications, offering unlimited design options and premium materials.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><strong>Cost</strong><span>: $500–$1,200+ per linear foot</span></li><li><strong>Details</strong><span>: Custom cabinets are made-to-order, requiring skilled craftsmanship and higher-quality materials. The higher cost reflects the labor-intensive process and tailored design.</span></li><li><strong>Best for</strong><span>: Luxury kitchen remodels or unique layouts that require bespoke solutions.</span></li></ul>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>If custom cabinets feel out of reach, semi-custom options can provide a personalized look at a more affordable price.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p>
<h2><span>What Should You Consider Before Replacing Your Kitchen Cabinets?</span></h2>
<h4><span><br/></span></h4>
<h3><span>How Does Kitchen Design Impact Costs?</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>The layout and design of your kitchen play a big role in the cost of replacing cabinets. A simple, straight-line design is less expensive to work with, while complex layouts with islands or corner cabinets can increase costs.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Here’s how design factors influence pricing:</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><strong>Straight-Line Kitchens</strong><span>: These are the most affordable layouts, as they require fewer cabinets and simpler installations.</span></li><li><strong>L-Shaped or U-Shaped Kitchens</strong><span>: Costs are higher due to the increased number of cabinets and the need for precise fitting in corners.</span></li><li><strong>Kitchens with Islands</strong><span>: Adding cabinets to an island increases both material and labor costs.</span></li></ul>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Plan your cabinet layout carefully to maximize storage and functionality while keeping costs manageable.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <h3><span>What Factors Influence the Final Cost of New Cabinets?</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Several elements determine the overall cost of replacing kitchen cabinets. Here are the key factors to consider:</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p> <ol><li><strong>Kitchen Size</strong><span>: Larger kitchens require more cabinets, increasing both material and labor costs.</span></li><li><strong>Cabinet Type</strong><span>: Stock cabinets are the cheapest, while custom cabinets are the most expensive.</span></li><li><strong>Material and Finish</strong><span>: Choices like solid wood and specialty finishes add to the total price.</span></li><li><strong>Labor Rates</strong><span>: Local labor costs vary by region, with urban areas generally charging more.</span></li><li><strong>Additional Features</strong><span>: Add-ons like soft-close hinges, pull-out shelves, or glass doors increase the cost.</span></li></ol>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Focus on features that add the most value to your kitchen while staying within your budget.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p>
