<p><span>Seeing a scratch or stain on your new tile can be disheartening. </span></p><p><span><br/>But with the right care and maintenance, you can keep your tiles looking new for years to come. </span></p><p><span><br/>Stick around as we dive into maintaining your tiles’ pristine condition and ensuring their longevity.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p>
<h2><span>Choose the Right Cleaning Products</span></h2>

<p><span>Tiles may look durable, but the type of cleaner you choose affects its longevity. It’s best to avoid products formulated with harsh chemicals. Contrary to common belief, pH-neutral cleaners are effective albeit gentle. </span></p><p><span><br/>However, in the case where you need to use stronger cleaners, do it only on tiles that can handle the extra strength without getting dulled or damaged.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p>
<h2><span>Keep Grout Clean</span><span><br/></span></h2>

<p><span>Grouts are magnets for dirt and grime, indeed. However, if you manage to keep it clean, it will help maintain the overall look of your tile. A clean grout line certainly enhances the looks of your flooring.</span></p><p><span><br/>To do this, try making a paste with baking soda and water. Apply it with a toothbrush, and for tougher stains, add a little hydrogen peroxide. </span></p><p><span><br/>Applying a sealant every year or two can prevent staining and cleaning easier. </span></p><p><br/></p><p><span><span>This is similar to the maintenance required for</span>
</span><a href="/granite-countertops-and-butcher-block-countertops">granite countertops</a><span>, where regular sealing is also essential.<br/></span></p>
<h2><span>Incorporate Low-Maintenance Routines</span><span><br/></span></h2>

<p><span>A little bit of daily care such as sweeping or mopping can go a long way. </span></p><p><span><br/></span><span>Keep it gentle, by only using soft materials like soft-bristle broom and a microfiber mop. </span></p><p><span><br/></span><span>Always sweep in one direction, and when you mop, use warm water. You won’t believe how much dirt you can get when you start using warm water, and not just soaking the tiles. </span></p><p><span><br/></span><span><span>You also have to be careful not to soak your tiles too much. Excess water can seep into grout lines, and this can cause damage.</span>
</span></p><p><span><span>﻿</span></span></p>

<h2><span>Spot Cleaning and Stain Removal</span></h2>

<p><span>The faster you address spills, the less likely they are to stain. Blot spills before cleaning the area with a mild detergent. </span></p><p><span><br/>For organic stains, baking soda is one of the best choices, while hydrogen peroxide can tackle mold and mildew.</span></p><p><span><br/>And if you are dealing with persistent stains, and can’t seem to identify where it’s coming from, it’s best to call a professional.</span></p><p><span><br/>They can provide deep cleaning or repair services that are beyond the scope of routine maintenance.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>For more comprehensive cleaning, you might also want to consider r</span><a href="/what-you-need-to-know-about-replacing-your-homes-carpet-with-luxury-vinyl-plank-flooring">eplacing your home's carpet with luxury vinyl plank flooring</a><span>.</span></p><p><span><span>﻿</span><br/></span></p>
<h2><span>Care for Different Tile Types</span><span><br/></span></h2>

<p><span><span>Different materials have their unique needs.</span>
</span></p><p><span><span>﻿</span></span></p>
<h3><span><span>Ceramic Tiles</span>
</span></h3>

<p><span>Regular sweeping, damp mopping with a mild cleaner, and immediate spill cleanup would be enough to maintain them. For this reason, ceramic tiles are renowned for their durability and ease of maintenance.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p>
<h3><span>Porcelain Tiles</span></h3>

<p><span>While similar to ceramic, it’s more dense, which makes them even more resistant to moisture and wear. It’s recommended to use a mild detergent and avoid abrasives to maintain their sleek finish.</span></p><p><span><br/></span></p>
<h3><span>Natural Stone Tiles</span><span><br/></span></h3>

<p><span>Natural stone tiles require a bit more TLC. It is advised to include regular sealing to prevent stains and water damage.
