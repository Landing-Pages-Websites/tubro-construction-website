<p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><span>Basic refresh (paint, fixtures, lighting): $5,000-$10,000</span></li><li><span>Mid-range remodel (new vanity, toilet, shower): $10,000-$20,000</span></li><li><span>Premium renovation (layout changes, high-end materials): $20,000-$40,000+</span></li></ul>
<p><span><br/></span></p> <h3><span>Common Bathroom Elements and Their Costs</span></h3>
<h4><span><br/></span></h4>
<h4><span>Fixtures and Features</span></h4>
<p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><span>Toilet: $200-$800+</span></li><li><span>Sink: $150-$900+</span></li><li><span>Vanity: $300-$3,000+</span></li><li><span>Shower: $1,000-$6,000+</span></li><li><span>Bathtub: $700-$5,000+</span></li><li><span>Flooring: $3-$20 per square foot</span></li><li><span>Lighting: $100-$400+ per fixture</span></li><li><span>Fan: $150-$500</span></li></ul>
<h4><span><br/></span></h4>
<h4><span>Labor Costs in Maple Valley</span></h4>
<p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><span>Plumber: $80-$120 per hour</span></li><li><span>Electrician: $75-$100 per hour</span></li><li><span>Tile setter: $60-$95 per hour</span></li><li><span>Contractor fee: 15-20% of total project cost</span></li></ul>

<p><span><br/></span></p> <ul><li><span>Basic bathroom permits: $150-$300</span></li><li><span>Electrical/plumbing permits: $50-$200 each</span></li><li><span>Inspections: Usually included with permits</span></li></ul>

<h3><span>Mid-Range Remodel ($15,000-$25,000)</span></h3>
<p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>With a mid-range budget, you can replace all fixtures and add some nice upgrades. </span></p><p><span><br/></span></p><p><span>Maybe you'll convert that old tub to a shower, put in new flooring, update the lighting, and get a new vanity with a stone top.
